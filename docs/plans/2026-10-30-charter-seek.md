# Bound-lineage charter seek and authority attendance

Continue the same pinned charter proposal and retained AM01–AM06/AM16 cases.
Add pure procedural projections for a bound lineage's record-citing filing,
declared recurrence/window and the authority's subsequent attendance deadline.
This is not an exemption from the zone's evaluation gate: filing remains an act.

Use authenticated actual rule/version, bound-rule inventory, complete breach
record identities/status/times, published route and filed anchor. Count distinct
current records under that rule, including records involving other affected
lineages; do not silently require that every breach belongs to the filer.
Below-threshold refusal reports the count without blaming the lineage and
preserves the council route. Empty citations are not a seek.

Attendance requires a bound actual publication: opening notice or a written
refusal answering the filed records. A late response does not erase the missed
deadline. Response adequacy/signatures, real published notices, complete
protected histories and the actual zone gate remain provider/governance work.
The private time-boundary choices must be explicitly installed rather than
inferred from a candidate. Outputs never execute or mutate anything. Review,
regression, installed wheel and push follow the existing checkpoint discipline.

Implemented scope uses positive integer rule versions and explicit private
window-start/deadline edge choices; these are installed semantic adapter inputs,
not new RFC wire fields or candidate-selected exceptions. Unknown/withdrawn/
out-of-window/misbound records add no eligible recurrence; duplicate original
record identities cannot multiply it. The provider must establish complete
current records and must not disguise an unavailable registry as an empty one.

Attendance and recourse are separate: an incomplete refusal may leave the duty
unmet while remaining contestable. A later notice preserves the earlier refusal
and its contest route. Qualified late attendance does not erase authority debt.
The translated AM16 chain composes these projections with forward amendment
metadata; it exercises procedural arithmetic, not a real vote, publication,
governor execution or installed-policy mutation.

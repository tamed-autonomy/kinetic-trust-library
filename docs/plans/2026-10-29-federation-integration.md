# Federation native integration

Pinned proposal: `8a733c4ab9e9063eb9cd64f92bc2cd34b4f84169`, especially
Federation Sections 1.4, 4.5, 5.1, 5.4 and 6.5. Forty source vectors are retained;
the whole suite is not yet implemented. No final RFC tag is adopted.

Implemented: distinct pure imported-standing result and shared bounded-standing
Core/capacity seam, receiver/class/lowering factor bounds and receiver software
ceilings. Do not substitute an extra cap on locally recomposed contributions.

Installed evidence checkpoint: item 1 and deterministic selected bilateral
software checks from item 2 now exist in `federation.py`/`federation_terms.py`.
The source owns actual signatures, current registry/acceptance, exportable earned
standing and forward-only version/admission history. One-link/current-validity
checks from item 4 are implemented for software standing attestations; other
record-delivery/person/multilateral routes and actual deployment adapters remain.
Items 3, 5 and full native producer/current-use composition are still pending.

Next implementation:

1. Install a closed current source for actual agreement/version/directional
   receiver signature, subject, sender/receiver, ingress time, action/substrate
   selectors, class ceiling, declared lowering policy, acceptance/revocation and
   validity. Authenticate a separate sender-locally-earned exportable amount;
   total foreign E_base alone is insufficient. Retain raw evidence and expiry.
2. Check complete agreement cells and current signatures, forward-only versions,
   direction-specific factor and admission-time applicability. A later factor
   version affects next ingress rather than rewriting an existing admission.
   Expiry/severance cannot be extended by cached proofs or sessions. Invalid
   clauses bind no act; their presence does not void unrelated agreement terms.
3. Add an explicit installed imported route. Keep local receiver identity/root
   and applicable ceiling validation. Feed the derived amount into local Core,
   then carry exact context/provenance/expiry through claims and geometry.
   Unavailable import evidence permits an independently selected local route;
   never synthesize a v2 fallback or permission from the foreign proof.
4. Enforce one-link attestation, actual witnessed registry-verified signatures,
   no re-export of imported standing, signed person acts only when provided for,
   no exported personal reconciliation records or veto events, and exposure-only
   breach delivery to reached zones. Anchoring notification is not local closure.
5. Add cross-zone bond checks: home-recorded reserve and total stake, sponsor
   admissibility at receiver measure, subject ceiling, actual prior breach counts,
   earliest bond/agreement/severance end, and independently retained earned standing.
   Service owns atomic reservations and actual delivery/penalty enforcement.
6. Verify actual signed producer/current-use and source cases, then require the
   selected contracts in complete deployment/API composition. Preserve explicit
   unsupported optional routes until implemented.

Unresolved draft details: exportable amount extraction from compound E_base and
combining imported standing with later locally earned standing. Require an
authenticated exportable amount and keep mixed accumulation outside this route
until a selected policy defines it. Informative old mesh/propagation examples do
not override the one-link rule or authorize foreign risk/decisions.
